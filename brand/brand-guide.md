# The Football Brief — Brand Guide

---

## Brand Concept

The Football Brief is positioned as the *breaking news desk of football analysis* — urgent, credible, and visually premium. The aesthetic borrows from classic newspaper front pages and modernises them for vertical video. Every frame should feel like a headline worth stopping for.

---

## Color Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary Background | Deep Black | `#0a0a0a` | Video overlays, text boxes, lower thirds |
| Accent | Antique Gold | `#c9a84c` | Player names, borders, logo ring, highlight elements |
| Secondary | White | `#ffffff` | Body stats text, caption text, clean contrast elements |

### Color Rules
- Never use pure white `#ffffff` as a background — it destroys the premium feel
- Gold is reserved for names, borders, and emphasis — do not overuse it
- Background overlays use black at 70–85% opacity to keep video visible underneath text

---

## Typography

### Title / Editorial Font
**Playfair Display**
- Use for: channel logo, series title cards, thumbnail headlines
- Weight: Bold or Black
- Style: Serif, editorial, newspaper authority
- Source: Google Fonts (free)

### Stats / Impact Font
**Impact** (primary) or **Oswald** (CapCut alternative)
- Use for: player names, stat numbers, short punchy labels
- Weight: Bold / ExtraBold
- Style: Condensed sans-serif, maximum legibility at small sizes
- Note: Use Oswald in CapCut where Impact is unavailable

### Caption Font
- Auto Captions in CapCut: **Bold** preset
- Color: White `#ffffff` with black stroke, or yellow — Bold style handles this automatically
- Position: Center screen (not bottom — Shorts UI covers bottom 20%)

## Logo Description

**Concept:** Newspaper masthead inside a stadium badge shape.

**Construction:**
- Outer shape: Perfect circle
- Background fill: Deep Black `#0a0a0a`
- Border: Antique Gold `#c9a84c` ring, 4–6px stroke weight
- Text: "THE FOOTBALL BRIEF" in Playfair Display Bold
  - "THE" — small caps, white, centered above main text
  - "FOOTBALL BRIEF" — large, white or gold, dominant masthead weight
- Optional secondary element: thin gold horizontal rules above and below text (classic newspaper separator)

**Usage:**
- Channel profile picture: logo centered in circle, no padding issues
- Watermark on videos: small version, bottom-right or top-right corner, 30–40% opacity
- Thumbnail overlay: full logo in upper-left corner at 15–20% of frame width

## Text Graphics Style for CapCut

### Player Name Graphic
| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Font | Impact (or Oswald as fallback) |
| Color | Antique Gold `#c9a84c` |
| Size | Large — approximately 80–100pt equivalent |
| Case | ALL CAPS |
| Position | Lower-center or center of frame |
| Shadow | Black, 4–6px offset, 60% opacity |
| Stroke | Black stroke, 2–3px, for legibility on bright video |

### Stats Box Graphic
| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Box Background | White `#ffffff` |
| Box Border | Antique Gold `#c9a84c`, 3–4px |
| Text Color | Deep Black `#0a0a0a` |
| Text Font | Oswald or Impact, Bold |
| Text Size | Medium-large — number large, label smaller below |
| Corner Radius | 4–8px (slight rounding, clean look) |
| Padding | 12–16px internal |
| Position | Center or slightly left/right of frame, avoid top 10% and bottom 20% |

**Example stat box layout:**
```
┌─────────────────────┐
│  [ GOLD BORDER ]    │
│   27                │
│   Goals This Season │
└─────────────────────┘
```

### Auto Captions Setup
| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Style | Bold preset in CapCut |
| Position | Center screen vertically (50–60% from top) |
| Max words per line | 3–4 words |
| Font | CapCut Bold auto style (handles its own font) |
| Color | White text, black outline — or yellow, per Bold preset |
| Background | None (stroke/shadow handles legibility) |

**Why center screen?** YouTube Shorts UI places subscribe buttons and interaction icons at the bottom 15–20% of the frame. Center placement gets maximum eyeballs and avoids overlap.

## Thumbnail Style (for non-Shorts content or Shorts cover)

- Background: Black `#0a0a0a` or cinematic still from the video
- Player image: full-bleed, high contrast
- Title text: Playfair Display Bold, white or gold, top-third of frame
- Accent bar: thin gold horizontal rule under title
- Channel logo: top-left corner, small

---

## Brand Voice

- **Direct.** No filler. Say the thing.
- **Confident.** Statements, not questions. We know football.
- **Fast.** Short sentences. One idea at a time.
- **Credible.** Real stats, real names, real stakes.
- **Not sensational.** Punchy but not clickbait. The facts are dramatic enough.

---

## What to Avoid

- Cluttered screens — maximum 2 text elements visible at once (caption + one graphic)
- Rainbow colors or neon — this is not a gaming channel
- Lowercase casualness in on-screen text — ALL CAPS for names, Title Case for labels
- Comic Sans, Helvetica Neue (too corporate), anything decorative or script
- Covering the subject's face with text boxes
