# Script Template — The Football Brief

Use this template for every Short. Do not deviate from the structure. The formula is what keeps retention high.

---

## Core Rules

- **Total length:** 25–45 seconds. Optimal: 38–42 seconds.
- **Word count target:** 90–130 words (ElevenLabs at 0.85 speed delivers ~3 words/sec)
- **Hook:** First sentence. Bold statement. Never a question. Never "Did you know...?"
- **One topic only.** No tangents. No padding.
- **CTA is always:** "Follow The Football Brief." — exact wording, every video.
- **Stats:** At least 2 per video. Real numbers. Surprising ones.

---

## Structure

### [HOOK] — First 3 seconds (~8–12 words)

A single bold declarative statement that forces the viewer to stop scrolling.

Rules:
- Makes a strong claim immediately
- No question marks
- No "Welcome back" or channel intro
- Must create instant curiosity or surprise

Bad: "Have you ever wondered how good Michael Olise really is?"
Good: "Michael Olise might be France's most dangerous player at this World Cup."

Bad: "Today we're talking about Neymar."
Good: "At 34 years old, this is Neymar's last shot at a World Cup winner's medal."

---

### [BODY] — Seconds 4–35 (~75–100 words)

Two to three short paragraphs. Maximum 2–3 sentences per beat. Build the argument.

**Beat 1 — Context / Setup (4–12 sec)**
Establish why this matters. One sentence of context.

**Stat 1 — First surprising number (12–18 sec)**
Drop the first stat. Make it land. Don't explain it to death — let the number speak.
Format: "[Number]. [Brief framing sentence]."

**Re-engagement line — 50% mark (~18–22 sec)**
A sentence that resets the viewer's attention. Works best as:
- A pivot ("But here's what nobody's talking about...")
- A tension-builder ("This is where it gets interesting.")
- A contrast ("And yet, France still aren't taking him seriously.")

**Stat 2 — Second surprising number (22–30 sec)**
Second stat, hits harder because of the re-engagement setup.

**Beat 2 — Payoff / Conclusion (30–37 sec)**
One or two sentences wrapping the argument. Bring it back to the stakes.

---

### [CTA] — Last 3 seconds (~10–12 words)

Exactly: **"Follow The Football Brief for daily World Cup 2026 coverage."**

Or short version: **"Follow The Football Brief."**

Never vary this. Brand recall depends on repetition.

## Word Count and Timing Reference

| Duration | Word Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 25 seconds | ~75 words | Very short — tight hook, 1 stat, hard CTA |

| 40 seconds | ~120 words | Optimal — full structure, comfortable pacing |
| 45 seconds | ~135 words | Maximum — do not go over |

## Timing Marks Format

When writing scripts, add timing marks in brackets. Example:

```
[0:00] Michael Olise might be France's most dangerous weapon at this World Cup.

[0:04] He finished the Bundesliga season with 15 goals and 10 assists for Bayern Munich.

[0:10] STAT: 15 goals, 10 assists — Bundesliga 2024/25.

[0:15] At just 23 years old, he's playing the best football of his career.

[0:19] But here's the thing nobody's saying —

[0:21] France might not even start him.

[0:24] STAT: Olise has just 8 senior France caps heading into this tournament.

[0:30] Deschamps has options. But the numbers say Olise deserves his chance.

[0:36] World Cup 2026. France. The winger debate starts now.

[0:39] Follow The Football Brief.
```

## Example Full Script

**Topic:** Michael Olise — France WC2026
**Target:** 40 seconds | ~120 words

```
Michael Olise might be France's most dangerous weapon at this World Cup.

He put up 15 goals and 10 assists at Bayern Munich this season — the best attacking numbers
of any French wide player in Europe's top five leagues.

But here's what makes this story interesting —

Olise only has 8 senior France caps. He's been brilliant at club level for two years,
and Deschamps kept looking elsewhere.

France's attack at this World Cup could be elite. Mbappé. Griezmann. And now Olise,
finally getting his shot on the biggest stage.

When he's on form, defenders cannot live with him.

Follow The Football Brief for daily World Cup 2026 coverage.
```

**Word count:** 116 words | **Estimated duration:** ~39 seconds at 0.85 speed

## Quick Checklist Before Saving Script as DONE

- [ ] Hook is a bold statement (no question mark)
- [ ] Hook lands in the first 3 seconds (~8–12 words)
- [ ] Body contains exactly 2 real, verifiable stats
- [ ] Re-engagement line is at roughly the 50% mark
- [ ] Word count is between 90 and 130
- [ ] CTA is "Follow The Football Brief" — exact wording
- [ ] Tone is direct and confident — no filler phrases
- [ ] No sentence exceeds 20 words (short sentences only)
- [ ] Script has been read aloud and sounds natural
