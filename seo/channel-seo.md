## Per-Video SEO Template

### Title Formula

```
[PLAYER/TEAM NAME]: [Bold Claim] | WC2026
```

**Examples:**
- `Michael Olise: France's Deadliest Weapon at WC2026`
- `Neymar's Last Dance — Can Brazil Win in 2026?`
- `Turkey vs Romania: Who Goes to the World Cup?`
- `Hakan Calhanoglu: The Best DM in Europe Nobody Talks About`

**Rules:**
- Keep titles under 60 characters (YouTube cuts off at ~55–60 in mobile search)
- Lead with the biggest name or boldest claim
- Include "WC2026" or "World Cup 2026" in every title for search indexing
- No clickbait — the title should describe what the video actually delivers
- No ALL CAPS titles — they look spammy in search results
- Use a colon or em dash to separate the subject from the claim

## Shorts-Specific Discoverability Best Practices

### Thumbnail (Cover Frame)
- YouTube auto-selects a frame from your Short as the cover
- Before upload, scrub to the most visually striking moment and manually select it
- Best frames: player name graphic visible, high-contrast background video, no caption text cluttering the frame
- The cover frame is what appears in the Shorts feed — make it stop-scroll worthy

### First 3 Seconds = Retention Hook
- YouTube's Shorts algorithm heavily weights watch-through rate
- If viewers bail in the first 2 seconds, the algorithm will not distribute the video
- The hook line in the script is also the SEO hook — front-load the key search term

### Hashtags
- Always use `#Shorts` as the first hashtag — this signals to YouTube's algorithm
- Use `#WorldCup2026` and `#Football` in every video
- Maximum 15 hashtags — beyond that YouTube ignores them

**Standard hashtag block for every video:**
```
#Shorts #Football #Soccer #WorldCup2026 #WC2026 #FIFA #FootballBrief
```
Then add 3–5 video-specific hashtags.

### Posting Time
- Upload at 18:00 Turkey time (UTC+3) daily
- This hits: Turkey/MENA at evening scroll, Western Europe at 16:00 CET, UK at 15:00, US East Coast at 10:00
- Consistency matters more than the exact time — same time every day trains the algorithm

### Engage in First Hour
- Reply to every comment in the first 60 minutes after upload
- Early engagement signals to YouTube the video is generating conversation
- Pin a comment with a question to drive more replies: "Who do you think France's best player is?"

### Series Cross-Promotion
- End description with: "Watch the full [Series Name] series: [link to playlist]"
- Create a playlist for each series and add videos as they upload
- Playlists increase session time, which improves channel authority
